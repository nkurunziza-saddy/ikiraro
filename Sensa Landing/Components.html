Section · Toolbar

The floating toolbar.

The signature Sensa control — sits over any page, dark capsule on cream. Each pill toggles a single assistive feature. Active state uses the ember accent.

Anatomy

  • Pill container border-radius: 9999px, ink fill, 6px inner padding
  • Buttons are 32 × 32, full round, transparent until hover
  • Active button = ember #C8442A with cream icon
  • Icons stroke 1.8, 15 × 15 inside the button
  • Optional vertical divider (1px hairline at 14% on-dark)

Section · Captions

Live caption bar.

The dark bar that pins itself to the bottom of any media surface and renders Sensa's live transcription. CC tag uses the ember stamp to mark the channel.

Anatomy

  • Background rgba(31,27,20,.94) — deep ink with translucency
  • 3px radius, 12 / 16 padding, drop shadow at sh-3
  • CC pill: solid ember, 10px uppercase letter-spaced
  • Body text 14px Inter on cream
  • Compact size drops to 13 / 8-12 padding

Section · Voice

The voice listening indicator.

A round pill that floats on the page while Sensa is listening for a command. The pulsing dot makes the state obvious without text.

States

  • Idle — muted stone dot, no animation
  • Listening — ember dot pulsing 1s ease-in-out
  • Recording — saturated ember, slower pulse
  • Body text in italic to read as live system speech, not chrome

Section · Controls

Switch & toggle row.

The control sitting next to every feature in the side panel. Switch in isolation is reusable; the full row pairs it with an icon, label, and short description.

Anatomy

  • Switch is 36 × 20, 9999px radius. Off uses the rule-tan, on uses ember.
  • Thumb is 16 × 16, cream, with 1px soft shadow. Slides 16px on toggle.
  • Toggle row: 32px icon pad on left, two-line label, switch right-aligned.
  • Description text in italic to soften it next to the label.

Section · Floating annotations

The annotation card.

Floats over hero imagery or product UI to call out a feature. Cream paper background, ember icon pad, two-line text with an italic detail line.

Anatomy

  • Cream card on subtle drop shadow, 3px radius, 12 / 16 padding
  • Icon pad 28 × 28, cream fill with hairline border, ember icon
  • Primary line: 13px medium ink
  • Detail line: 11px italic stone, hangs as a quote
  • Max width 360px before clipping
