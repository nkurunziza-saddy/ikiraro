Style guide · corne.rs × Inter
lisse /lēs/ adj.  [F. lisse, smooth]
1 having an even, unbroken surface; smooth to the touch.
2 a sleek; without break or rough patch.
b (of a curve) continuous; without abrupt change.
3 fig. polished, frictionless; flowing without interruption.
Typography

Inter at weight 300 for body, 400 for UI, 500 for headings only. The thinness does the work — restraint at the weight level reads as sophistication. No size needs to go above 20px.
